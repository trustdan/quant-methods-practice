## Published module objectives (learner-supplied, October 11, 2026)

Question coverage against these objectives is tracked in [CONTENT-BACKLOG.md](CONTENT-BACKLOG.md).

| Module | Learning objectives |
|---|---|
| 1 | Explain the importance of statistics in business. Calculate and explain measures of center and variability. Explain key probability concepts and apply probability rules to business problems. |
| 2 | Define a discrete random variable and its probability distribution. Solve problems using expected value and variance; apply rules for transformations of random variables. Define, choose between and use Poisson and binomial distributions for business problems. |
| 3 | Differentiate discrete and continuous distributions. Define the form and parameters of the normal and exponential distributions. Choose between and use them to model business problems. |
| 4 | State and apply the CLT for the mean (and proportion). Explain how sample size affects standard error. Construct a CI for a population mean (and proportion). Compute the sample size required for a desired margin of error. |
| 5 | Describe hypothesis-testing steps. Formulate H₀ and H₁. Define Type I and Type II errors. Test a mean (or proportion) and report the p-value. Explain one-sample vs. two-sample tests. |
| 6 | Recognize the form and assumptions of simple and **multiple** regression. Develop a regression equation for prediction. Assess significance and fit (p-value, R², standard error of estimate). Interpret the slope CI and the significance of each predictor. Identify common regression problems. |
