# Evidence, transfer and scheduling

This is a transparent practice-selection heuristic, not a validated memory probability. Persist attempts/exposure and derive projections. Inject clock/RNG, compute decay at read time and handle clock rollback without inflation.

## Evidence policy v0

Designate at most one evidence stage per concept per instance. An independent first success adds one success, an independent first error adds one error. Hinted retry never erases the first error. Pre-answer hints, reference use, earlier answer reveal or guided contrast make dependent responses assisted. Review, saved-note reading and free tutor conversations do not award success. Duplicate commands and repeated substeps cannot add counts.

Start with alpha=1,beta=1, base=alpha/(alpha+beta), configurable three-day half-life, retention factor 2^(-elapsed_days/half_life_days). New concepts display new, not demonstrated 50% mastery. Show counts, recency and assistance alongside any priority score. Persist evidence-policy version and rebuild rather than overwriting historical grades.

## Transfer and scaffolds

Reviewed setting groups bind exact template versions to meaningful reasoning conditions. Number/name changes share a group. Intermediate guidance initially requires at least two independent successes, score >=.60, successes in two groups, and delayed group-changing success. Faded requires at least three successes and score >=.80 with the same transfer requirement. An error restores full guidance. Thresholds are configurable design defaults, not scientific guarantees.

Initial delayed criterion is >=10 minutes since both previous independent response and latest relevant exposure, including hints/notes/assisted practice. This measures a scheduling condition, not proven long-term retention; later release evaluation should use longer intervals. Keep half-life fixed initially; any adaptation needs sufficient delayed evidence, bounded changes and policy versioning.

Historical attempts without reviewed setting metadata retain their original results but receive no invented transfer credit. Updating pedagogy version cannot manufacture a new setting. Assisted comparison problems remain assisted at every stage.

## Selection

Use seeded weighted selection with a nonzero eligibility floor, new/weak concepts, elapsed time, current verified scope and a roughly 20% mixed-review allocation. Penalize recent exact templates and reviewed groups. Full mode may offer unseen concepts with manageable examples; intensive mode changes density rather than pretending rapid repetition is delayed retrieval.

After an eligible misconception, queue an approved active contrast partner as the next available session problem, preserve compatible parameters, use full guidance, and mark contrast assistance. Do not extend session indefinitely or chain contrasts. Missing/incompatible/retired partners fall back to normal selection.

Acceptance: repeated variants cannot graduate a concept; reading a saved answer blocks immediate independent credit for the relevant scenario; decay changes display/priority without rewriting evidence; replay is stable; skipped problems do not invent success or failure.
